**Registration rules (mandatory):**

- **What to register:** ① setting nouns; ② every numeric fact — timelines ("X years ago"), character ages, durations, amounts, headcounts; ③ planted foreshadowing and signature through-lines (register at first use)
- **Register before you write:** if the text needs a number or a planted clue, and it isn't already in this table, register it first. **Never invent numbers on the spot** — mismatched timelines across three chapters are almost always caused by on-the-fly figures
- **Deliver on schedule:** any entry with a "Planned reveal" chapter must pay off when you reach that chapter, and the "Reader knows" column must be updated as the chapter closes; a foreshadowing you decide to abandon must be logged as "dropped," never left floating
